Drafts of some thoughts

* Building and understanding systems is itself a human intellectual activity worth preserving.
* We don’t yet know whether “art” names a property of an object, an effect on an audience, an intentional human activity, or some combination of these. AI forces us to notice that those things used to arrive bundled together, so we didn’t have to distinguish them.
* The client is also human. Businesses aren’t actually terminal sources of value. Eventually all this optimization bottoms out in things humans care about: security, comfort, curiosity, status, beauty, connection, time, meaning, play.
